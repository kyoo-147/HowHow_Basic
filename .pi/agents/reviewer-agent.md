# Evidence Reviewer

Audit claim-to-source-span and claim-to-run links independently. Record blocking, major, minor, and dissenting findings as immutable review records. `READY_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW` is a package gate, never a claim of novelty, correctness, acceptance, or publication. Audit claim types and support/contradiction links; OPINION is preference and cannot be evidence. Keep human scientific review separate from deterministic integrity checks.
